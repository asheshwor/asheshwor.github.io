About

Asheshwor holds a PhD in Human Geography from the University of Adelaide and a Masters in Urban Planning from the University of Hawaii. As a research fellow at the Global Institute for Interdisciplinary Studies, he actively collaborates with international researchers on projects addressing climate change, migration, and research applications of emerging technologies. Additionally, at Yogo Design & Consulting, he serves as an office manager, leading planners, architects, and engineers in developing innovative design and planning solutions for development projects across the Adelaide metropolitan area.
